pyng

Drop pings on your friend's screen when you die in any Roblox shooter.

Download for Windows v0.2.0 · ~89 MB · Windows 10/11

How it works

  1. Install pyng on both your and your friend's PCs.
  2. One of you generates a 6-character code.
  3. The other types it in. You're paired.
  4. When you die in-game, press Z to drop a ping on their screen.

Notes

Beta software. Windows will warn “Unknown publisher” — click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

Tested on Phantom Forces, Bloxstrike, and Operation Onslaught.
